## 1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. Similar technologies include local storage and pixels. We call them all "cookies" here.

## 3. Your choices

When you first visit, a banner lets you accept or reject non-essential cookies. You can change your choice at any time through the "Cookie settings" link in the footer, or by clearing cookies in your browser. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may stop the dashboard working.
