# Vegam Privacy Policy

> Draft for lawyer review. Not legal advice.

**Version:** 0.1 (draft) · **Effective date:** [date]

This Privacy Policy explains how **[Vegam legal entity]**, of **[registered address]** ("Vegam", "we", "us"), handles personal data.

## 1. Two roles

**When we decide how data is used (we are the data fiduciary / controller).** This covers:
- visitors to our website;
- people who request a demo, contact us, or try our demo assistant;
- owners, managers and staff of businesses that use Vegam ("Users").

**When we act for a business (we are a data processor).** Businesses use Vegam to reply to, call, book and follow up with their own customers ("End Customers"). For End Customer data, **the business is the data fiduciary / controller** and decides how the data is used. We process it only on the business's instructions, under a [Data Processing Agreement](data-processing-agreement.md). If you are an End Customer, please contact the business you dealt with first; we will help them respond to you.

## 2. Data we collect

| Who | Data | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Website visitors | IP address, device and browser data, pages visited, cookies | Automatically (see [Cookie Policy](cookie-policy.md)) |
| Demo and contact requests | Name, business name, phone, email, message | You |
| Users | Name, email, phone, role, login and activity records | You or your employer |
| Customers (billing) | Billing contact, business details, tax ID (e.g. GST), payment status (card or UPI details are handled by our payment provider, not stored by us) | You and our payment provider |
| End Customers (as processor) | Name, phone number, social media ID, messages, call recordings and transcripts, bookings, answers to questions, consent and opt-out records | The End Customer and the business |

We do not knowingly collect data about children under 18. Businesses must not use Vegam to process children's data unless a verifiable parental consent process is in place and agreed with us.

## 3. How we use data (as data fiduciary / controller)

| Purpose | Legal basis |
|---|---|
| Provide accounts, support and the Service to Users | Contract; legitimate uses under DPDP Act; contract (GDPR art. 6(1)(b)) |
| Respond to demo and contact requests | Consent; legitimate interests (GDPR art. 6(1)(f)) |
| Billing, tax and accounting | Legal obligation; contract |
| Security, fraud prevention, abuse detection | Legitimate uses; legitimate interests; legal obligation |
| Improve the website and Service using aggregated or de-identified data | Legitimate interests; consent where required |
| Product updates and marketing to business contacts | Consent, or legitimate interests where allowed; you can opt out at any time |

We do not sell personal data. We do not use End Customer data to train general-purpose AI models.

## 4. Sharing

We share personal data only with:
- **Sub-processors** that host or help run the Service (cloud hosting, database, AI model providers, messaging and telephony providers, email, monitoring, payments). The current list is in our [Sub-processor List](subprocessors.md).
- **The business** you interacted with, if you are an End Customer.
- **Authorities**, where required by law or to protect rights, safety and security.
- **A buyer or successor** in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, under equivalent protections.

## 5. International transfers

Vegam is a global service. We aim to host data in a region suited to each business [India for Indian customers]. Some sub-processors (for example AI model providers) may process data in other countries. Where we transfer data across borders, we follow the law of the place it came from, including any restrictions notified under India's DPDP Act, and, for data from the EU/UK, appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

## 7. Security

We use encryption in transit and at rest, access controls, row-level separation of each business's data, audit logs and monitoring. See our [Security and Incident Response Policy](security-and-incident-response.md). If a personal data breach occurs, we will notify affected businesses, individuals and authorities as required by law.

## 8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:
- access the personal data we hold about you and a summary of how it is processed;
- correct, complete or update it;
- erase it;
- withdraw consent at any time (this does not affect processing already done);
- object to or restrict processing, and data portability (EU/UK);
- know, delete, correct, and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information (California and other US states); we do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising;
- nominate another person to exercise your rights if you die or become incapacitated (India);
- complain to a regulator, such as the Data Protection Board of India or your local data protection authority.

To exercise your rights, contact us using the details below. We will verify your identity and respond within the time the law requires. End Customers should contact the business first; if you contact us, we will pass your request to that business.

## 9. Messaging and calls

If a business uses Vegam to message or call you, you can stop messages by replying **STOP** (or the equivalent in your language), and ask for a human at any time. Voice calls answered by Vegam's AI announce that you are speaking to an AI assistant and ask before recording.

## 10. Changes

We will post changes here and update the effective date. For material changes, we will notify Users by email or in the dashboard.
