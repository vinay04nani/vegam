# Founder IP Assignment Deed (template)

> Template for later use. Draft for lawyer review. Not legal advice. Use this when Vinay Kumar forms a company (for example a private limited company) and wants the company to own Vegam, which investors and acquirers normally require. Under the Copyright Act, 1957, the assignment must be in writing and signed. Stamp duty may apply depending on the state; your lawyer will advise.

## Background

A. The Assignor conceived, designed and developed "Vegam", an AI front desk software product, including its source code, documentation, product plans, designs, specifications, prompts, data models, brand names and logos, and related materials (the "Vegam IP"), before the Company was incorporated.

B. The Assignor wishes to assign the Vegam IP to the Company, and the Company wishes to accept it, on the terms below.

## 1. Assignment

1.1 In consideration of **[₹[amount] / the allotment of [number] equity shares in the Company / other]**, the receipt and sufficiency of which the Assignor acknowledges, the Assignor assigns to the Company absolutely all rights, title and interest, throughout the world, in the Vegam IP, including:

- all copyright in the software, documentation and other works, for the full term of copyright, for all media and modes of exploitation;
- all rights in the "Vegam" name, logo and other trademarks, with the goodwill attached to them, and any applications to register them;
- all rights in confidential information, know-how and trade secrets relating to Vegam;
- the GitHub repository `vinay04nani/vegam` [and any domains, social media accounts and third-party accounts listed in the Schedule]; and
- the right to sue for past, present and future infringement.

1.2 This assignment is not subject to the default limits in section 19 of the Copyright Act, 1957: it is worldwide, for the full term of copyright, and does not lapse if the rights are not exercised within one year.

## 2. Moral rights

To the extent permitted by law, the Assignor agrees not to assert moral rights in the Vegam IP in a way that prevents the Company from using, modifying or commercialising it.

## 3. Assignor's confirmations

The Assignor confirms that, to the best of their knowledge:
- the Assignor is the sole owner of the Vegam IP, or has obtained written assignments from all contributors;
- the Vegam IP has not been assigned, licensed or charged to anyone else;
- no third-party claims are pending against it.

## 4. Further assurance

The Assignor will sign any documents and do anything the Company reasonably requests to register, perfect or enforce its rights in the Vegam IP, including transferring trademark applications and copyright registrations.

## 5. Governing law

This Deed is governed by the laws of India. The courts at Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh, have exclusive jurisdiction.
