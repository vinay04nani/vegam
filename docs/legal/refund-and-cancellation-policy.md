# Vegam Refund and Cancellation Policy

> Draft for lawyer review. Final terms depend on the pricing and plans agreed after pilots.

**Version:** 0.1 (draft) · **Effective date:** [date]

This policy applies to subscriptions and services bought from **[Vegam legal entity]** and forms part of the [Terms of Service](terms-of-service.md).

## 1. Pilots and trials

Pilots and free trials are free of charge unless agreed otherwise in writing. No payment is taken, so no refund applies. At the end of a pilot, the Service stops unless you choose a paid plan.

## 2. Cancelling a subscription

- **Monthly plans:** you may cancel at any time from the dashboard or by emailing [billing email]. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing month. You keep access until then.
- **Annual plans:** you may cancel renewal at any time before the renewal date. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the annual term.

## 3. Refunds

- **Monthly fees:** fees already paid for the current month are not refunded, except as stated below.
- **Annual fees:** if you cancel within [14] days of the start of a new annual plan, we will refund the annual fee minus the monthly price for the time used. After [14] days, annual fees are not refunded, except as stated below.
- **Setup and onboarding fees:** non-refundable once setup work has started.
- **Usage fees** (AI call minutes, marketing messages and similar): non-refundable once used.
- **Service failure:** if we materially fail to provide the Service and do not fix it within [30] days of your written notice, we will refund prepaid fees for the unused period.
- **Material changes:** if we materially reduce a paid feature during your term and you cancel because of it, we will refund prepaid fees for the unused period.
- **Duplicate or incorrect charges:** refunded in full.

## 4. How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are paid to the original payment method within [7–10] working days of approval. Timing after that depends on your bank or payment provider.

## 5. Suspension and termination by us

If we suspend or end your account for breach of the [Acceptable Use Policy](acceptable-use-policy.md) or non-payment, no refund is due.

## 6. Your data after cancellation

You can export your data for [30] days after cancellation. After that it is deleted, as described in the [Data Processing Agreement](data-processing-agreement.md).
