# Contributor IP Assignment and Confidentiality Agreement (template)

> **Template only. Not legal advice.** Have a lawyer in India review and adapt this before anyone signs it. Under the Indian Copyright Act, 1957 (sections 18 and 19), an assignment of copyright is valid only if it is **in writing and signed** by the person assigning it, so each contributor must sign a copy (on paper or by a valid electronic signature). Keep every signed copy.

## 1. Purpose

The Owner has created and owns Vegam, an AI front desk software product, including its source code, documentation, designs, product plans and related materials (the "Project"). The Owner wishes to give the Contributor access to the Project so that the Contributor can help develop or scale it.

## 2. Assignment of work

2.1 All work the Contributor creates for, or in connection with, the Project, including code, designs, documents, inventions, improvements and ideas (the "Work"), belongs to the Owner from the moment it is created.

2.2 The Contributor hereby assigns to the Owner all present and future rights, title and interest in the Work, including all copyright, worldwide, for the full term of those rights, for all media and modes of exploitation.

2.3 The Contributor waives, to the extent permitted by law, any moral rights in the Work, and will not object to the Owner modifying, adapting or publishing it without attribution.

2.4 The Contributor will sign any further documents the Owner reasonably needs to register or enforce these rights.

## 4. Confidentiality

4.1 The Contributor will keep confidential all non-public information about the Project and the Owner's business, including source code, product plans, pricing, customers and strategy ("Confidential Information").

4.2 The Contributor will use Confidential Information only to work on the Project for the Owner, and will not disclose it to anyone without the Owner's written consent.

4.3 These duties continue for **[5] years** after this Agreement ends, and indefinitely for source code and trade secrets.

## 5. No competing use

During this Agreement and for **[12] months** after it ends, the Contributor will not use the Project or Confidential Information to build, help build or work on a competing product.

## 6. Third-party material

The Contributor will not include in the Work any code or material that the Contributor does not have the right to assign, or that is licensed under terms (such as GPL or AGPL) that would restrict the Owner's proprietary use, without the Owner's written approval.

## 7. Access and ending

7.1 Access to the Project is personal, non-transferable and revocable at any time by the Owner.

7.2 When access ends, the Contributor will return or delete all copies of the Project and Confidential Information and confirm this in writing.

7.3 Sections 2, 4, 5 and 8 survive the end of this Agreement.

This Agreement is governed by the laws of India. The courts at Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh, have exclusive jurisdiction.
