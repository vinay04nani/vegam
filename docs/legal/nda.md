## 1. Purpose

The Parties wish to share confidential information to evaluate and discuss **[a possible collaboration / investment / partnership relating to Vegam]** (the "Purpose").

## 2. Confidential Information

2.1 "Confidential Information" means all non-public information shared by one Party (the "Discloser") with the other (the "Recipient"), in any form, including product ideas and concepts, business plans, pricing, customer and prospect information, source code, designs, specifications, prompts, data and know-how, whether or not marked confidential.

2.2 Confidential Information does not include information that the Recipient can show:
- is or becomes public without the Recipient's fault;
- the Recipient already lawfully knew without a duty of confidence;
- the Recipient lawfully receives from a third party without a duty of confidence; or
- the Recipient develops independently without using the Discloser's information.

## 3. Obligations

The Recipient will:
- use Confidential Information only for the Purpose;
- keep it confidential and protect it with at least reasonable care;
- share it only with its employees or advisors who need it for the Purpose and are bound by confidentiality duties at least as strict as these;
- not copy, reverse engineer, or create products or services based on it;
- tell the Discloser promptly about any unauthorised use or disclosure.

## 4. Legally required disclosure

If the law or a court requires the Recipient to disclose Confidential Information, it may do so, but must (where allowed) give the Discloser prompt notice and disclose only what is required.

## 5. No rights granted

All Confidential Information remains the Discloser's property. Nothing in this Agreement grants any licence or right to any intellectual property, or obliges either Party to enter into any further agreement.

## 6. Return or destruction

On the Discloser's request, or when discussions end, the Recipient will return or destroy all Confidential Information and confirm this in writing.

## 7. Non-solicitation [optional]

For [12] months after this Agreement, neither Party will solicit the other's employees or contractors introduced through the Purpose.

## 8. Term

This Agreement applies to information shared from the date above. The confidentiality duties last **[5] years** from the date of disclosure, and indefinitely for source code and trade secrets.

## 9. Remedies

Unauthorised disclosure may cause irreparable harm. The Discloser is entitled to seek injunctive relief in addition to any other remedy.

## 10. Governing law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of India. The courts at Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh, have exclusive jurisdiction.
