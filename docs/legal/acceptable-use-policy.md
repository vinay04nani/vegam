# Vegam Acceptable Use Policy

> Draft for lawyer review. Not legal advice.

**Version:** 0.1 (draft) · **Effective date:** [date]

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") is part of the Vegam [Terms of Service](terms-of-service.md). It applies to every business that uses Vegam and to all its Users. We may suspend or end access for any breach.

## 1. Contact only people who reached out or agreed

You must not use Vegam to:

- send messages or make calls to anyone who has not contacted your business, is not your existing customer, or has not given the consent required by law;
- make cold calls, buy or import contact lists for outreach, or message people scraped from the internet;
- contact anyone after they have opted out;
- call people outside the time window and calling hours allowed by law (for example, in India, only within the period allowed after a recorded enquiry, unless you have explicit recorded consent, and only from properly registered number series);
- make AI reminder calls or promotional calls where your number type or registration does not allow them.

Vegam blocks many of these automatically. Trying to get around those controls is a breach of this AUP.

## 2. Follow channel and telecom rules

You must:

- follow the WhatsApp Business Terms and Policies, Meta Platform Terms and Instagram/Messenger policies, including opt-in, template and 24-hour window rules;
- keep your messaging quality high and stop campaigns if your quality rating drops;
- complete any telecom registration your country requires (for example DLT registration in India) and use only permitted number series;
- comply with anti-spam and telemarketing laws (for example TRAI TCCCPR in India, TCPA and CAN-SPAM in the US, PECR and ePrivacy in the UK and EU).

## 3. Be honest

You must not:

- configure the AI to claim to be a human, or remove its AI disclosure;
- record calls without the notice and consent required by law;
- give false or misleading information about prices, offers or availability;
- impersonate another person or business.

## 4. Prohibited content and uses

You must not use Vegam for:

- illegal goods or services, fraud, scams, phishing or pyramid schemes;
- harassment, threats, hate speech or abusive content;
- sexually explicit content or adult services;
- gambling, betting or lending where not licensed, or in a way that breaks the law;
- weapons, drugs, or other regulated goods without a licence;
- political campaigning or election messaging;
- debt collection that is threatening, deceptive or breaks the law;
- medical, legal or financial advice or diagnosis by the AI;
- collecting children's data without verifiable parental consent;
- collecting sensitive personal data (such as health records, government ID numbers, card numbers or passwords) through chats or calls, unless agreed with us in writing.

## 5. Protect the Service

You must not:

- attempt to access other customers' data or bypass security, rate limits or usage limits;
- attempt to extract the AI's system prompts or misuse the AI (prompt injection, jailbreaking) to generate prohibited content;
- reverse engineer, copy or resell the Service;
- overload the Service, send malware, or interfere with its operation;
- share login details or let unauthorised people use your workspace.
