# Vegam legal documents

> **Drafts for lawyer review. Not legal advice.** These documents are working drafts based on general knowledge of Indian law (DPDP Act 2023 and DPDP Rules 2025, IT Act 2000, Copyright Act 1957, TRAI TCCCPR), the EU/UK GDPR, US privacy and telemarketing laws, and Meta's WhatsApp Business policies. Laws in this area changed in 2025–2026 and keep changing. Have a qualified lawyer in each launch country review every document before it is published or signed.

Everything in `[square brackets]` must be filled in or confirmed before use.

## Before publishing: checklist

- [ ] Decide the contracting entity: Vinay Kumar as an individual / sole proprietor, or a registered company. Update every `[Vegam legal entity]` placeholder.
- [ ] Registered address, contact email, and Grievance Officer name and contact.
- [ ] Final product name and domain (Vegam is a working title).
- [ ] Sub-processor list matches the providers actually in use.
- [ ] Data hosting regions confirmed (do not claim "data stays in India" until it is true).
- [ ] Lawyer review in India; add country-specific review before selling in the EU/UK, US or elsewhere.
- [ ] Publish the customer-facing documents at stable URLs and record the version and effective date of each.
- [ ] Keep signed copies of every DPA, NDA and IP assignment.

## Versioning

Every published document carries a version and an effective date. When a document changes, keep the old version in git history, update the date, and notify customers as the Terms require.
