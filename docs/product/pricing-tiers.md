## 1. Starter: "Never miss a message"

| What's included | How it works | Use case |
|---|---|---|
| **AI replies on WhatsApp, 24/7** | The business's WhatsApp number is connected to Vegam. The AI answers only from the business's own information (services, prices, timings, policies), in the customer's language. | A customer asks "How much for a consultation? Free tomorrow evening?" at 11 PM and gets the price and an open slot in 5 seconds. |
| **Missed call → WhatsApp** | Calls the business can't answer are forwarded to Vegam. The caller gets a WhatsApp within seconds with options to book or ask for a callback. | The owner is busy with a customer, a new caller rings out, and gets "Sorry we missed you. Book here or reply CALL." |
| **Booking page + calendar** | Customers book in chat or on a Vegam booking link (website, Instagram bio, Google profile, QR code). Staff can add phone and walk-in bookings. | A customer books a slot through WhatsApp without anyone at the business touching a phone. |
| **Reminders with Confirm / Reschedule** | One reminder the day before. The customer taps Confirm, Reschedule or Cancel; the calendar updates itself. | No-shows drop; a customer who can't make it reschedules by tapping a button instead of just not turning up. |
| **Website lead capture** | Contact-form enquiries from the business's existing website reach Vegam (email forward or one-line snippet). A WhatsApp button and QR code are included. | Someone fills the website form at night and gets a WhatsApp reply immediately instead of a call two days later. |
| **One automatic follow-up** | If a customer goes quiet after asking prices or seeing slots, Vegam sends one polite nudge. It stops on reply, booking or STOP. | "Asked the price and disappeared" turns into a booking the next day. |
| **Team inbox (2 seats)** | Every chat in one place. Staff can take over any conversation; the AI pauses for that chat. | A customer complains; the AI hands over, and the manager replies personally. |
| **Morning report** | One WhatsApp message to the owner at 8 AM. | "Yesterday: 14 enquiries, all replied. 6 booked. 2 missed calls recovered. 3 chats need you." |
| **Allowances** | 1,000 AI conversations a month. Meta's WhatsApp template fees billed at cost. | |

## 2. Growth: "Every channel and every call"

**Everything in Starter, plus:**

| What's included | How it works | Use case |
|---|---|---|
| **AI answers incoming calls (300 min)** | When staff don't pick up, the AI voice agent answers, says it's an AI assistant, answers questions, books a slot and transfers to a person when needed. Every call gets a summary. | During the evening rush the AI answers 11 calls, books 5 and passes 2 urgent ones to the owner. |
| **Instagram, Facebook Messenger and website chat** | All channels land in the same inbox, answered by the same AI. | A "price?" Instagram DM gets the price list and a booking link instantly. |
| **Ad lead forms** (Meta and Google) | New ad leads are contacted within a minute on WhatsApp. | Leads the business paid for are answered while they're still interested. |
| **3-step follow-up + lead scoring** | Quiet leads get up to three gentle nudges. Each lead is marked Hot, Warm or Cold based on their answers and behaviour. | Staff start the day calling the 5 hottest leads instead of scrolling chats. |
| **Google review requests + AI review replies** | After a visit, happy customers are asked for a Google review; unhappy ones reach the owner privately first. The AI drafts replies to reviews. | The business gets more 5-star reviews, which brings more local enquiries. |
| **Calendar sync** (Google Calendar, Calendly) | Bookings from their existing tools flow into Vegam for reminders and reports. | A business already using Calendly keeps it; Vegam handles reminders and no-shows. |
| **Owner's WhatsApp assistant** | The owner texts Vegam: "Bookings today?", "Block Friday 2–5 PM", "Pause the AI". | The owner runs the front desk from their phone without opening a dashboard. |
| **Weekly "what Vegam won you" report** | Bookings, recovered calls and estimated value for the week. | The owner sees the return on the subscription in their own numbers. |
| **Allowances** | 5 seats · 3,000 AI conversations · 300 voice minutes a month. | |

## 3. Pro: "Grow and bring customers back"

**Everything in Growth, plus:**

| What's included | How it works | Use case |
|---|---|---|
| **AI calls new leads** | Leads who enquired are called by the AI within a minute, only within the legal contact window and permitted hours. | A website enquiry at 10 AM gets a call at 10:01 and is booked before they contact a competitor. |
| **Payment links + payment reminders** | Send UPI or card payment links for deposits, invoices and balances. Polite reminders on day 3, 7 and 14 stop once paid. | Deposits cut no-shows; overdue bills are collected without the owner chasing. |
| **Broadcasts** (opted-in customers only) | Send offers, festival greetings or announcements to chosen customer groups. | "Diwali offer: 20% off this week" reaches 400 regular customers in one go. |
| **Win-back + rebooking reminders** | Customers who haven't returned get a gentle message; regulars get "time for your next visit" reminders. | A customer who usually visits every 6 weeks gets a nudge at week 6 with a booking link. |
| **Multi-branch** | One inbox and calendar for several locations, with chats routed to the right branch and reports per branch. | A business with three locations manages all front desks from one place. |
| **Integrations** | Google Sheets, Zoho, Tally, Shopify and more. | Every new booking appears in the accountant's sheet automatically. |
| **Custom AI voice and persona + priority support** | The AI uses the business's chosen name, tone and voice. Priority setup and support. | The AI introduces itself as "Asha from [Business]" in a warm, formal tone. |
| **Allowances** | Unlimited seats · 1,000 voice minutes a month. | |
