# Vegham: product overview

Vegham is an AI voice agent that answers a business's calls and messages and remembers every person it talks to.

## What it does

- **Voice:** picks up calls in a natural voice, can be interrupted, answers from what the business taught it.
- **Languages:** answers in the language the customer starts in and switches when they do.
- **Long memory:** keeps a short record of each person (name, language, preferences, unfinished conversations) and uses it the next time they get in touch.
- **Channels:** phone calls, WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, Telegram and a shareable link. Voice works on phone calls and the link; the others are chat (WhatsApp and Telegram also take voice notes).
- **One person across channels:** a call and a WhatsApp from the same number are the same customer. Chats on other channels join when the person shares their number.
- **Hand-over:** when a conversation needs a person, it says so and passes it on with a summary.
- **Follow-ups:** keeps track of who to get back to, and drafts the message.
- **Knowledge:** learns from the website, files and notes; follows "always" and "never" rules.
- **Insights:** what was answered, who was recognised, what people asked.

## Screens

Website: landing, product, how it works, pricing, company, careers, contact, book a demo, security, privacy, terms, help centre.

Account: sign up, log in, code entry.

Set-up (after sign-up): your business, pick a voice, test call, go live.

App: Home, Conversations, People, Follow-ups, Insights, and under "Your agent": Agent, Languages, Memory, Knowledge, Channels, Test call. Settings opens as a pop-up from the profile menu.
