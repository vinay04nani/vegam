Do and don’t

  • Aurora sits behind content, soft and blurred
  • One bold aurora moment per page
  • Ink for primary actions, outline for secondary
  • ✕Pastel fills or coloured containers
  • ✕Glow directly under small text
  • ✕Emoji as icons; mixed icon styles
