Pricingquestions

Which plan should I start with?

Most businesses start with Growth because it answers calls as well as messages. If customers mainly reach you on WhatsApp, Starter is enough. Use the plan picker above, or ask us.

How does the 30-day pilot work?

In week 1 we set you up and measure today's baseline: replies, missed calls and bookings. In weeks 2–4 Vegham runs your front desk and you see the difference in your morning report. On day 30 you choose a plan or stop, and your data is yours to export.

What counts as an AI conversation?

Everything one customer says to Vegham within 24 hours counts as one conversation, however many messages. For example, asking the price, picking a slot and confirming is one conversation.

How are voice minutes counted?

Each minute the AI spends on a call, rounded up to the nearest minute. A 2½ minute booking call uses 3 minutes.

Who pays WhatsApp's fees?

Replies within 24 hours of a customer's message are free. Reminders and follow-ups sent after that use Meta's message templates, which have a small fee per message. We pass these on at cost.

Are taxes included?

Prices in rupees are shown before 18% GST, which is added to your invoice. Prices in dollars are for customers outside India.

What happens if I go over my allowance?

Nothing stops working. Extra AI conversations are ₹1.5 ($0.02) each and extra voice minutes ₹6 ($0.08) each, and we'll tell you before you reach your limit.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. Move up or down at any time; changes apply from your next billing period.

Can I cancel?

Yes. There's no lock-in on monthly plans. You can export your contacts and conversations before you leave.
