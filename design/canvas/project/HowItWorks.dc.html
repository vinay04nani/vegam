Setupquestions

How long does setup take?

About an hour of your time on Day 1. Connecting WhatsApp needs Meta’s approval, which is often quick but can take up to a few days.

Do I need technical skills?

No. Every step is guided, and we can do it with you on a call.

Do I need a new WhatsApp number?

Often not: many numbers can keep using the WhatsApp Business app alongside Vegham. If yours can't, we'll help you set up a separate number. We check this during setup.

Will customers know they’re talking to an AI?

Yes. Vegham says it's an AI assistant when asked and at the start of every call, and hands over to a person whenever someone asks.

Does Vegham make up answers?

No. It answers only from the information you approve. When it isn't sure, or a customer is upset, it hands the chat to your team.

What happens after the 30 days?

You choose a plan, or stop. Nothing is charged without your say, and you can export your data.
