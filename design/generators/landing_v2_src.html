What Vegham does

A front deskthat never sleeps.

01 / 05

Replies in seconds

Answers prices, timings and questions on WhatsApp, Instagram and your website, only from what you’ve taught it.

  • English, Hindi and mixed messages
  • Sends the chat to you when unsure
02 / 05

Answers every call

A natural voice picks up, books visits and transfers to your team when a caller asks for a person.

  • Missed calls get a WhatsApp in seconds
  • Every call summarised
03 / 05

Books and reminds

Checks your calendar, confirms the visit and reminds the customer the day before.

  • Confirm / Reschedule replies
  • Works with Google Calendar and Calendly

  • Win-back for opted-in customers

  • Weekly results summary
