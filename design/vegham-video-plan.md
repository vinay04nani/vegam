# Vegham launch film: pitch, storyboard and Higgsfield prompts

## The pitch (what the film has to say)

**One line:** Never miss a customer again. Vegham answers your calls and messages, and remembers every person it talks to.

**Problem:** A missed call or an unanswered message is a customer who went elsewhere. Small teams can't pick up everything, and nobody remembers what a caller said six months ago.

**What Vegham is:** One AI voice agent for your business. It picks up, talks naturally in the caller's language, and remembers people for months.

**Three things to land, in this order:**
1. **It answers.** Every call, every message, day or night.
2. **It remembers.** "Hi Rohan, good to hear from you again. Last time you asked about weekend sessions." Long memory, long context.
3. **It's everywhere.** Phone, WhatsApp, Instagram, Telegram, a link on your site. Wherever your customers are, anywhere in the world.

**Close:** the lowercase wordmark in aurora gradient, "Never miss a customer again.", vegham.ai

**Rules the film keeps:** no industry named, no invented customer quotes or statistics, wordmark only (no logo symbol), no sparkle or star "AI" icons.

## Look

- Deep near-black background with slow aurora light: #10B981 → #0EA5A4 → #3B6CF6 → #7C3AED → #DB2777. Same gradient as the website hero and wordmark.
- Aurora green (#10B981) is the "voice": the sound wave and the thread of memory are green.
- Clean white type, Inter Tight for headlines.
- Calm, confident pacing. No fast cuts, no glitch effects.

**Practical note:** video models garble on-screen text and distort brand logos. Generate every shot **without text or logos**, then add the captions, the vegham wordmark and the real WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram logos in the edit.

## Higgsfield prompts, shot by shot

Recommended model: **Seedance 2.0** (4–15 s, 16:9 and 9:16, 1080p) or **Cinema Studio Video 3.0** (4–15 s, up to 4K). Both are in your Higgsfield catalog. Turn generated audio **off**; music and voice go on in the edit. To keep the look consistent, generate shot 2 first and use its last frame as the start image or a reference image for the others.

Add this style line to the end of every prompt:

> Style: cinematic, deep near-black background, slow flowing aurora light in emerald green, teal, blue, violet and magenta, soft volumetric glow, shallow depth of field, premium minimal technology film, smooth slow camera move, no text, no letters, no logos, no people's faces.

**Shot 1 (4 s):** A smartphone lying on a dark stone counter in an empty room at dusk. The screen lights up with an incoming call and the phone vibrates gently. Nobody comes. Faint aurora glow through a window in the background. Slow push-in toward the phone.

**Shot 2 (4 s):** A ribbon of aurora light drifts down through the dark and flows into the smartphone. On the screen a glowing emerald-green sound wave wakes up and begins to pulse calmly, as if answering. Light spreads across the counter. Slow push-in.

**Shot 3 (5 s):** Two horizontal sound waves floating in dark space, facing each other like a conversation. The left one is white, the right one is emerald green. They take turns pulsing. Halfway through, the green wave smoothly shifts hue toward teal and back, suggesting a change of language. Slow sideways drift.

**Shot 4 (6 s):** A single thin thread of emerald-green light travels through a vast dark space, passing through small glowing orbs spaced far apart, like moments on a timeline. The camera glides forward along the thread from the first orb to the last, which glows brightest. Aurora haze in the distance.

**Shot 5 (5 s):** A stream of aurora light enters from the left and splits into five separate streams. Each stream reaches a floating rounded glass tile and lights it from within: five tiles in a gentle arc. The tiles are blank frosted glass. Slow orbit around the arc.

**Shot 6 (3–4 s):** The Earth at night seen from low orbit. Fine arcs of aurora-coloured light connect city lights across continents, appearing one after another. A real aurora shimmers along the horizon. Slow rotation.

**Shot 7 (4 s):** Aurora light settles into a calm, soft glow along the bottom of a pure black frame, leaving the centre empty and dark. Very slow drift. Hold.

For the vertical cut, re-generate shots 1, 2, 4 and 7 with the same prompts at 9:16.

## Edit checklist

1. Join the shots in order with soft cross-dissolves (about 8 frames).
2. Add captions in white Inter Tight, lower third, one line each.
3. Shot 5: place the real phone, WhatsApp, Instagram, Telegram and link icons on the five glass tiles.
4. Shot 7: lowercase "vegham" wordmark in aurora gradient, tagline below, vegham.ai.
5. Voiceover: warm, calm, unhurried. Music: soft ambient pad, one gentle rise at shot 4.
6. Export 1080p 16:9 for the website and YouTube; 9:16 for Reels and WhatsApp status.
